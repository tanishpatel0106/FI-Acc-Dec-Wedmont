# Substantive project progress

Updated November 24, 2026. Installation and GitHub publication are operational records and are excluded from the contributions below.

- Implemented four-wave SCF measurement and retrospective chronological prediction tests. Adding age improved willingness and liquidity-coverage predictions in all three held-out waves.
- Implemented household-budget stress tests for market losses and earned/all-income interruptions. The older-age advantage under earned-income interruption fell from 16.5 to 0.4 percentage points after income-composition adjustment and was no longer statistically significant.
- Estimated adjusted willingness/resilience relationships and reran independent SHED prospective and within-person analyses. Emergency savings predicted fewer subsequent unpaid bills; a causal willingness effect was not established.
- Reproduced Italian, Indian and European numerical results in a separate environment. The 13-country evidence inventory retains different evidence levels; harmonized national capacity estimates remain unavailable for several countries.
- Aligned public/WRDS comparisons and evaluated allocation rules on disjoint paths. The matched age-plus-cash-flow rule did not establish a CE improvement over age-only allocation.
- Corrected zero-balance portfolio continuation, horizon/uncertainty calculations and cache semantics. Re-evaluated the complete legacy demonstration and documented nine corrected forward summaries.
- Compiled repeated optimization helpers without changing their algorithms. Independent analytical/SLSQP checks passed; the recorded warm helper benchmark was approximately 4.2 times faster.
- Reproduced 16 numerical artifacts independently. The recorded validation suites passed 130 Python checks, 230 frontend unit tests and 26 browser tests. These checks support the implementation; statistical findings come from the empirical analyses.

Environment installation, the Windows installer, artifact packaging and GitHub upload are not counted as completed project contributions. Historical installation/publication receipts are retained solely for operational traceability.
